The business brain

Ask any business question. Get an answer with evidence.

Not a generic chatbot. The intended intelligence layer investigates the authorised business context, connects related signals and explains what the records support.

01 / ASKNatural language
⌁

Ask the question that matters

Ask about revenue, profit, customers, products, pricing, marketing, operations, team performance, risks or a decision you are considering—in plain language.

02 / INVESTIGATECross-business analysis
◉

Investigate the whole picture

Analyse relevant records across orders, expenses, customers, leads, bookings, products, campaigns and business memory. Connect causes and effects instead of returning an isolated metric.

03 / EXPLAINEvidence trace
⌖

Show why the answer is true

Present calculations, source records, assumptions, confidence and missing information. Never disguise an estimate as a verified result or invent unavailable data.

04 / DIAGNOSERoot causes
✧

Find the real bottleneck

Identify risks, weak points, unusual changes and missed opportunities. Explain likely root causes and what additional evidence would confirm them.

05 / DECIDEScenario analysis
◇

Compare possible decisions

Evaluate options, trade-offs, costs, constraints and scenarios. Show the assumptions behind projections so owners can make informed choices.

06 / IMPROVEAction loop
⟳

Turn insight into controlled action

Create tasks for specialist agents and automation workflows. Track approvals and outcomes, then use verified results to inform the next analysis.

Product vision and capability target—not a claim that every question, connector or advanced reasoning workflow is already production-ready. Answers depend on connected data, permissions and tested intelligence services.

Clear answers before you begin

Built for trust, not big promises.

Business tools should explain what they know, what they do not know and what they need permission to do.

Does Business Doctor AI work without historical business data?

Yes, the intended onboarding supports new businesses and existing businesses with partial or no historical records. When history is missing, the system should establish a baseline from the day you start rather than invent past performance.

Will it publish posts or send messages automatically?

Important actions are designed to remain under owner control. Social publishing, customer messaging, paid campaigns and other consequential actions should only happen after the required connection, permissions and approval are in place.

Are the figures in the website demo real?

No. Figures and charts in the public product preview are illustrative examples. They are not customer results, live analytics or a promise of business growth.

Does the AI make up missing sales or profit numbers?

It should not. Recommendations and reports must be grounded in available, verified records. Unknown or incomplete information should be clearly labelled.
