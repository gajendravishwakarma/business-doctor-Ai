Privacy Policy

Business Doctor AI
Effective date: September 15, 2026

Business Doctor AI ("Business Doctor AI", "we", "us", or "our") provides an AI-powered business operating system for businesses to understand their operations, manage customers and leads, analyze business data, and use approved business automations.

Important: We design the service around business-data privacy, tenant isolation, human approval for consequential actions, and protection of connected-account credentials.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how a business uses the service, we may collect:

  • Account and business information such as name, business type, contact details, country, timezone, and currency.
  • Customer, lead, booking, order, product, service, inventory, and expense information entered or imported by a business.
  • Files and business records uploaded for analysis, such as CSV, Excel, PDF, invoices, receipts, and reports.
  • Information from connected business services and APIs, only to the extent authorized by the business and supported by the relevant provider.
  • Website information when a business asks us to analyze a website.
  • Technical, security, audit, and usage information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

2. Connected Services

A business may choose to connect services such as WhatsApp Business, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, payment providers, or other supported business systems. When a connection is authorized, we process the information made available through that connection according to the permissions granted and the applicable provider's terms and policies.

Business Doctor AI does not ask users to provide passwords, one-time passwords, private API keys, access tokens, or other secret credentials through ordinary chat messages. Sensitive credentials received by the application are intended to be protected and masked where applicable.

3. WhatsApp Business Data

When a business connects WhatsApp Business to Business Doctor AI, the service may process business phone-number information, WhatsApp Business Account information, message events, customer messages, delivery/status events, and related metadata required to provide the requested functionality.

WhatsApp data is used to provide the connected business features, such as receiving customer inquiries, creating or updating relevant business records, providing AI-assisted support, and handling approved responses. We do not sell WhatsApp message content to third parties.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and secure Business Doctor AI.
  • Understand a business's data and generate evidence-based business insights.
  • Operate CRM, sales, booking, inventory, reporting, and automation features.
  • Provide AI-assisted recommendations and responses based on available business information.
  • Process authorized integrations and webhooks.
  • Detect errors, abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and security incidents.
  • Maintain audit records and enforce access controls.
  • Improve reliability and product functionality.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. AI Processing

Business Doctor AI may use artificial intelligence and third-party AI infrastructure to analyze information and generate business insights or drafts. AI-generated output is intended to be based on available business data. When reliable supporting information is unavailable, the service may indicate that there is insufficient data rather than intentionally presenting an unsupported business claim.

Certain consequential actions may require explicit business-owner or authorized-user approval before execution.

6. Data Sharing

We may share or disclose information only as reasonably necessary to operate the service, including with infrastructure, hosting, security, analytics, communications, AI, payment, and integration providers that process data on our behalf or at the business's direction.

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, security requirements, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of our service and users.

7. We Do Not Sell Business Data

Business Doctor AI does not sell business customer data or connected WhatsApp message content to third parties.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include authenticated access, role-based authorization, tenant-level data isolation, audit logging, credential protection, and security controls around integrations and webhooks.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should protect their own accounts and avoid sharing confidential credentials.

9. Business Data Ownership and Control

Businesses remain responsible for the data they provide to the service and for ensuring that they have the appropriate rights and permissions to process customer or employee information.

Subject to applicable law and the service's technical capabilities, businesses may request access, correction, export, or deletion of their information and may disconnect supported integrations.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and audit records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the business's use of the service.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Business Doctor AI may use cookies, local storage, sessions, or similar technologies required for authentication, security, preferences, and service functionality. If optional analytics or similar technologies are introduced, their use will be handled according to the applicable settings and notices.

12. Children's Privacy

Business Doctor AI is a business service and is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating business accounts.

13. Third-Party Services

14. International Processing

Depending on the infrastructure and service providers used, information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where a business operates. We take reasonable steps to use appropriate safeguards for such processing where required by applicable law.
